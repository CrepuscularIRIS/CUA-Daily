title: IntReal: A Real-World Dataset and Benchmark for Multimodal Interleaved Generation
source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38425

§Abstract
Humans routinely consume multimodal content, where textual and visual information is naturally interleaved. Although Unified Multimodal Models (UMMs) can jointly generate text and images, multimodal interleaved generation remains largely underexplored. Progress in this area is hindered by two key limitations: (1) the lack of well-defined interleaved generation tasks and corresponding training data, and (2) the absence of fine-grained evaluation protocols that can reliably diagnose model performance across different capability dimensions. To this end, we present IntReal, comprising two complementary components: IntReal-100K, a real-world dataset of multimodal interleaved samples spanning four major categories, and the IntReal Evaluation Suite, which evaluates model performance across seven complementary dimensions using specialized expert models, enabling coarse-to-fine diagnosis. Extensive experiments validate our evaluation suite through low inter-metric redundancy (max pairwise absolute Spearman's $ρ\leq 0.42$ ), high agreement with human judgments (83.3% accuracy), and strong consistency across different expert evaluators (Spearman's $ρ> 0.9$ ), supporting the complementarity and reliability of our metrics. By benchmarking four families of UMMs, we show that IntReal Evaluation Suite reveals distinct strengths and weaknesses across models. Post-training experiments demonstrate the utility of IntReal-100K for improving interleaved generation and further show that training unimodal generation can also benefit interleaved generation. These findings suggest that interleaved generation can benefit from modality-specific optimization, rather than requiring joint text-image training at every stage.

Encyclopedic articles interleave text and images to communicate knowledge, storybooks integrate narratives with vivid illustrations, tutorials pair step-by-step instructions with corresponding visual demonstrations, and travel guides present itineraries alongside maps and photographs. Despite the emergence of Unified Multimodal Models (UMMs) capable of generating both text and images, multimodal interleaved generation remains largely treated as a by-product of model unification rather than a fundamental generative task in its own right ( Deng et al., 2025 ; Cui et al., 2025 ; Diao et al., 2026 ) . A major reason for this underexploration is the lack of datasets and benchmarks specifically designed for this task. On the data side, as shown in table 2 , large-scale interleaved datasets such as MMC4 ( Zhu et al., 2023 ) , OBELICS ( Laurençon et al., 2023 ) and OmniCorpus ( Li et al., 2025b ) are primarily collected for multimodal understanding rather than generation, and therefore do not provide generation-oriented quality control. CoMM ( Chen et al., 2025c ) aggregates interleaved data sources, but does not organize data collection around a taxonomy of real-world scenarios, leaving the task space of multimodal interleaved generation insufficiently characterized. This field thus lacks a high-quality interleaved generation dataset constructed under a principled taxonomy. On the evaluation side, as shown in table 2 , existing benchmarks often rely on vision-language models (VLMs) to holistically assess long interleaved responses ( Zhou et al., 2025b ; Liu et al., 2024 ) , which is inherently misaligned with the fine-grained evaluation required to characterize complex generative capabilities ( Huang et al., 2024 ) . Recent studies improve evaluation granularity through modality-specific decomposition or structured verification ( Li et al., 2026b ) . For example, ISG-Bench ( Chen et al., 2025a ) decomposes responses into four levels of structural granularity, but this decomposition does not map onto distinct capability dimensions. Moreover, it relies solely on a VLM evaluator across all levels, which may lack the specialized competence some dimensions require. To address these limitations, we introduce IntReal, a real-world dataset and benchmark tailored to multimodal interleaved generation. We first identify four core generation scenarios from real-world interleaved content and organize them into a taxonomy. Guided by this taxonomy, we apply both understanding-oriented and generation-oriented filtering to a million-scale pool of raw interleaved data, resulting in IntReal-100K , a high-quality real-world dataset containing 538K images. Building on IntReal-100K, we further develop the IntReal Evaluation Suite , an evaluation framework grounded in real-world scenarios. Specifically, we construct an evaluation set through stringent automated filtering and expert curation. We then evaluate interleaved generation in two complementary settings: (1) holistic generation of complete interleaved responses to assess open-ended capabilities and (2) next-image generation conditioned on interleaved contexts for controlled diagnosis. Across both settings, we integrate specialized expert evaluators, such as image quality assessment (IQA) models, into seven metrics, enabling fine-grained diagnosis from diverse perspectives. We conduct extensive experiments to assess IntReal Evaluation Suite and the utility of IntReal-100K for training UMMs. First, we examine three properties of the suite. Its seven metrics show low redundancy (maximum pairwise absolute Spearman correlation below 0.42), suggesting that they capture distinct capability dimensions.
