title: PointerBench: A Diagnostic Benchmark for Fine-Grained GUI Grounding
source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31274

§Abstract
Multimodal Large Language Models have shown promising performance in computer-use tasks, from planning user workflows to grounding natural-language actions to precise UI targets. Existing end-to-end benchmarks primarily emphasize long-horizon task completion, while GUI grounding benchmarks remain focused on conventional controls and a limited set of professional interfaces. We introduce POINTERBENCH, a diagnostic suite of 1,500 screenshot-instruction pairs probing three complementary capabilities: thin sub-element identification and geometric grounding in spreadsheets (POINTERBENCH-Sheets), character-level grounding in long-text documents (POINTERBENCH-Text), and mixed GUI grounding across 100 professional applications (POINTERBENCH-Pro). We carefully curate each task with pixel-exact target geometry and evaluate both general-purpose multimodal models and 39 specialist GUI grounding configurations. We find text grounding to be the most challenging setting, with Claude Fable 5, GPT-5.6 Sol, and the leading native specialist UI-Venus-2-9B achieving 69.6%, 53.4%, and 25.8% success on POINTERBENCH-Text despite respective 83.2%, 77.7%, and 54.5% average success across POINTERBENCH. These results reveal substantial headroom in fine-grained GUI grounding across frontier and specialist models. We publicly release the benchmark and evaluation code at https://github.com/warmwindOS/pointerbench.

§Introduction (from cached fulltext; cut at ~9000 chars)
Computer-use agents have emerged as promising day-to-day assistants, automating workflows across browsers, desktop applications, mobile devices, and specialized professional software ( Deng et al., 2023 ; Zhou et al., 2024 ; Xie et al., 2024 ; Rawles et al., 2025 ; Li et al., 2025 ) . Multimodal agents interleave reasoning and action ( Yao et al., 2023 ) , combining long-horizon planning ( Erdogan et al., 2025 ) , external tool use ( Schick et al., 2023 ; Qin et al., 2024 ) , and visual grounding ( Cheng et al., 2024 ; Gou et al., 2025 ) . For tasks such as “open the meeting chat” or “paste cell B14 to cell E15”, successful execution requires both selecting an appropriate action sequence and grounding each step to precise screen coordinates or regions ( Zheng et al., 2024 ; Gou et al., 2025 ) . This remains challenging in modern graphical user interfaces (GUIs), which often contain dense and visually ambiguous interaction targets, as well as in high-resolution professional applications with small target regions ( Li et al., 2025 ; Wu et al., 2025 ) . Existing evaluations measure complementary but incomplete aspects of this problem. Interactive benchmarks such as Online-Mind2Web, WebArena, and OSWorld evaluate long-horizon task completion in websites or executable desktop environments ( Xue et al., 2025 ; Zhou et al., 2024 ; Xie et al., 2024 ) . They are essential for studying planning and recovery, but end-to-end success conflates failures in reasoning, grounding, execution, and environment control. Static benchmarks such as ScreenSpot and ScreenSpot-Pro isolate localization and have accelerated progress on visual GUI agents ( Cheng et al., 2024 ; Li et al., 2025 ) . Yet they lack fine-grained actions such as placing a cursor on a spreadsheet boundary, between two characters, or around a narrowly specified text span. Rapid progress on these benchmarks motivates complementary tests that vary the geometry of the requested action, diagnosing an agent’s ability to perform precise control. We introduce PointerBench , a fine-grained diagnostic benchmark comprising three 500-example tasks designed to diagnose these missing capabilities (Figure 1 ). PointerBench -Sheets probes cells, headers, colors, relative references, edges, resize handles, and corners under varied sheet layouts. PointerBench -Text evaluates points and bounding boxes over words, characters, punctuation, carets, and invoice fields in six languages. PointerBench -Pro covers icon, text, and mixed targets across 100 professional applications. All inputs use a fixed 1024\!\times\!768 coordinate frame and ship with executable scorers. Our contributions are twofold: (i) a diagnostic benchmark suite that isolates structural, textual, and professional GUI grounding through three complementary tasks with precisely defined target geometry; and (ii) a comprehensive evaluation spanning both general-purpose multimodal models and specialist GUI grounding configurations, revealing non-trivial rank reversals across subsets and a persistent bottleneck in character-level and region-level textual grounding.
