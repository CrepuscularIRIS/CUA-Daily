title: SC2Arena and StarEvolve: Benchmark and Self-Improvement Framework for LLMs in Complex Decision-Making Tasks
source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.10428

§Abstract
Evaluating large language models (LLMs) in complex decision-making is essential for advancing AI's ability for strategic planning and real-time adaptation. However, existing benchmarks for tasks like StarCraft II fail to capture the game's full complexity, such as its complete game context, diverse action spaces, and all playable races. To address this gap, we present SC2Arena, a benchmark that fully supports all playable races, low-level action spaces, and optimizes text-based observations to tackle spatial reasoning challenges. Complementing this, we introduce StarEvolve, a hierarchical framework that integrates strategic planning with tactical execution, featuring iterative self-correction and continuous improvement via fine-tuning on high-quality gameplay data. Its key components include a Planner-Executor-Verifier structure to break down gameplay, and a scoring system for selecting high-quality training samples. Comprehensive analysis using SC2Arena provides valuable insights into developing generalist agents that were not possible with previous benchmarks. Experimental results also demonstrate that our proposed StarEvolve achieves superior performance in strategic planning. Our code, environment, and algorithms are publicly available.

§Introduction (from cached fulltext; cut at ~9000 chars)
The rapid advancement of large language models (LLMs) ( Achiam et al. 2023 ; Guo et al. 2025 ) has accelerated research towards artificial general intelligence ( Mon-Williams et al. 2025 ; Zheng et al. 2025 ) . A critical milestone in this pursuit is the development of agents capable of strategic planning and real-time adaptation in complex environments. StarCraft II, a renowned real-time strategy game, provides an ideal testbed with its profound strategic depth, diverse unit types and interactions, vast decision space, and inherent demands for world modeling, as illustrated in Fig. 1 . These characteristics align closely with the LLMs’ core strengths ( Wei et al. 2022 ; Yao et al. 2023 ; Wang et al. 2023b ) , making it particularly suitable for LLM evaluation. Therefore, establishing benchmarks in StarCraft II to assess LLM performance is critical. However, existing LLM benchmarks in StarCraft II ( Ma et al. 2024 ; Li et al. 2024 ) often fail to capture the original game’s complexity, lacking support for full-length game context, complete low-level action spaces, comprehensive race configuration, and agent-vs-agent mechanisms. This limits their ability to evaluate the strategic reasoning and adaptability of LLM agents. To bridge this gap, we propose SC2Arena , a comprehensive StarCraft II benchmark for evaluating LLMs’ decision-making ability. With a standardized JSON interface, it supports ① full-length game context, ② all playable races (Terran, Protoss, Zerg), and ③ complete low-level actions, preserving the game’s strategic depth. Crucially, SC2Arena addresses inherent challenges of text-based agents by optimizing observations: proximity-based unit ordering captures units’ spatial relationships, and worker aggregation reduces information overload. Additionally, it enables agent-vs-agent gameplay, facilitating direct competition and an agent ranking mechanism to better evaluate performance and strategic adaptability. Furthermore, we introduce StarEvolve , a closed-loop agent framework that enables self-correction and self-improvement for strategic decision-making. It employs a hierarchical architecture: a Planner generates high-level strategic commands, while an Executor converts them into precise low-level actions. Both components integrate Verifier modules that perform iterative self-correction to ensure syntactic correction and feasibility. Crucially, StarEvolve achieves continuous self-improvement by collecting gameplay data and performing supervised fine-tuning (SFT). We design an RL-inspired scoring function to prioritize high-quality training samples, which quantifies action impact through discounted value increments across key game-state metrics. Utilizing these data, we fine-tune the Planner, Executor, and Verifiers to continuously improve their performance. In summary, our contributions are twofold: (1) We propose SC2Arena, a comprehensive benchmark for evaluating LLMs in StarCraft II, supporting full-length game context, all races, full action spaces, and agent-vs-agent gameplay. (2) We propose StarEvolve, a closed-loop agent framework, which integrates strategic planning with tactical execution. It achieves self-correction with iterative decision refinement, and also continuous self-improvement via SFT, enabling LLM agents to defeat challenging opponents.
