title: GroundGUI: GUI Grounding by High-Quality Data and Distillation-to-Reinforcement Learning
source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19100

§Abstract
Constructing robust GUI agents relies on multimodal visual grounding: the ability to precisely map natural language intents to actionable on-screen components. Despite abundant desktop, web, and mobile benchmarks, high-fidelity resources tailored for diverse environments are surprisingly scarce. To bridge this gap, we introduce the Screen Referring Grounding Dataset (ScreenRef), a comprehensive GUI grounding dataset consisting of GUI screenshots paired with natural language referring expressions and box or point annotations. Spanning desktop, web, and mobile domains, ScreenRef comprises 586K screenshots and over 633K referring expression instances, covering tasks such as text recognition, visual matching, spatial understanding, and refusal scenarios. To boost the model's grounding performance, we propose the Distillation-to-Reinforcement Learning (D2RL) method to address the limited generalization of supervised fine-tuning and the sparse reward problem in reinforcement learning. Utilizing ScreenRef, we train Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct via sequential SFT and D2RL training, yielding GroundGUI-8B. GroundGUI-8B achieves 95.2% on ScreenSpot-V2, 63.9% on ScreenSpot-Pro, 65.4% on OSWorld-G, 86.8% on MMBench-GUI L2, and 38.3% on UI-Vision. Among 8B-scale models, it produces substantial gains over models trained on open-source datasets, with performance comparable or even superior to models trained on closed-source data. Code, models, and datasets are available at https://github.com/PrentisAI/GroundGUI.

§Introduction (from cached fulltext; cut at ~9000 chars)
Graphical User Interfaces (GUIs) ( Cheng et al., 2024 ) are ubiquitous in modern human-computer and mobile interactions. Visual GUI grounding (mapping language instructions to pixel coordinates of interactive elements) is a foundational capability. Robust cross-platform grounding is challenging due to interface complexity and contextual diversity. Modern applications often have high-resolution canvases with dense layouts and subtle visual disambiguations, complicating spatial localization. GUI agents degrade on domain-specific software due to limited grasp of specialized icon semantics. Data underpins model capability, but constructing high-quality grounding datasets with broad coverage is challenging ( Xu et al., 2024 ; Xie et al., 2025 ) . Mobile-Agent-v3.5 ( Xu et al., 2026 ) , UI-Venus-1.5 ( Gao et al., 2026 ) , UI-TARS-2 ( Wang et al., 2025a ) , and MAI-UI ( Zhou et al., 2025 ) perform well with proprietary large-scale data, unavailable to the community. GroundCUA ( Feizi et al., 2026 ) and ScaleCUA ( Liu et al., 2026b ) release their data, but models trained on them are suboptimal due to limited diversity/accuracy. We introduce ScreenRef, a large-scale GUI grounding dataset (586K screenshots and 633K referring expressions), with text recognition, visual matching, spatial understanding, and refusal tasks. It covers desktop, web, and mobile platforms, with image resolutions ranging from 0.1 to 8.3M pixels. Multi-stage filtering/verification yield verified data for robust cross-platform grounding. Supervised fine-tuning (SFT) is commonly used to train grounding models, but overfits to specific domains ( Luo et al., 2025 ) . Reinforcement learning (RL) works ( Tang et al., 2026 ; Li et al., 2026 ; Liu et al., 2026a ; Lu et al., 2026 ) use Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) ( Shao et al., 2024 ) to improve generalization, but suffer from sparse signals on hard samples where all rollouts receive zero reward. Inspired by on-policy self-distillation (OPSD) ( Zhao et al., 2026a ) which provides dense token-level supervision, we propose Distillation-to-Reinforcement Learning (D2RL) , a two-stage post-training framework: OPSD followed by REINFORCE Leave-One-Out (RLOO) ( Ahmadian et al., 2024 ) . OPSD tailored for GUI grounding delivers rich token-level supervision for precise coordinate generation from a teacher that sees the ground-truth bounding box, providing a stronger starting policy before RL. RLOO then enhances model generalization and localization accuracy. Through the proposed ScreenRef and D2RL method, we obtain GroundGUI for precise GUI grounding across desktop, web, and mobile. GroundGUI-8B achieves 95.2% on ScreenSpot-V2 ( Wu et al., 2025b ) , 63.9% on ScreenSpot-Pro ( Li et al., 2025 ) , 65.4% on OSWorld-G ( Xie et al., 2025 ) , 86.8% on MMBench-GUI L2 ( Wang et al., 2025d ) , and 38.3% on UI-Vision, remarkably outperforming the models trained on open-source datasets. GroundGUI-8B also surpasses Holo2-8B ( H Company, 2025 ) and Step-GUI-8B ( Yan et al., 2025 ) by a large margin, both of which are trained on closed-source data. Core contributions. (1) ScreenRef , a high-quality GUI grounding dataset with 586K screenshots and 633K referring expressions, providing text recognition, visual matching, spatial understanding, and refusal supervision for robust GUI agents. (2) D2RL , which proposes OPSD before RL to shape a better initial policy, unleashing the potential of RL for GUI grounding, enhancing model generalization and grounding accuracy. (3) GroundGUI-8B , trained on our proposed data and approach. GroundGUI-8B achieves state-of-the-art or competitive performance among models with 8B parameters or fewer, surpassing several 32B-scale models, e.g., ScaleCUA-32B, OpenCUA-32B and GTA1-32B. We release the data, code, and model to the community to facilitate future research.
