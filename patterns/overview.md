# Computer-use agent patterns — overview

18 main patterns grouping 75 sub-patterns (55 candidate_pattern, 20 case). 72 come from two frozen Open Scientist domains: `cua-agents/v1` (K01–K35: world models, simulators, reward, task synthesis, training, planner–grounder, memory, evaluation) and `cua-agents-core/v1` (C01–C37, renumbered from that domain's K01–K37: perception and grounding inputs, agent loop and context, benchmarks and evaluation, security, cross-domain action tokenisation). The other 3 (R01–R03, domain `board-induced`, 2026-10-19) were induced from the wave-1 board's weak fits (32 of the first 100 scored papers) and each opens a new main pattern, leaving the success conditions and failure modes of the 15 existing mains unchanged. Every sub-pattern belongs to exactly one main. Sub-pattern cards copy the domain cards faithfully, with arXiv#signature citations. No outcome labels exist in this build, so there are no accept rates; success conditions and failure modes are reading-derived. See also [anti-patterns](anti-patterns.md).
