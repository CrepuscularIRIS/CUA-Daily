# K07 — Harvest a transition corpus by goal-free exploration plus VLM captioning
_parent: **Learn GUI Dynamics and Train in Them** ([`learn_and_train_in_gui_world_model`](../main/learn_and_train_in_gui_world_model.md)) · status: candidate_pattern · source card: `K07-goal-free-exploration-transition-harvest.md` (frozen domain `cua-agents/v1`)_

## Operation
random-walk or structured-walk real web pages/apps without tasks, deduplicate and filter transitions, and have a large VLM caption each before/after pair with the action and resulting change, producing supervision verified by real execution rather than teacher imagination

- Transfer: Analogue of random/novelty-driven data collection for dynamics learning; also overlaps with GUI-testing random exploration (Monkey-style), but output is captions not coverage.

- Status: candidate_pattern

## When it works
- Heuristics that favour clicks/new elements; scroll actions dropped as unhelpful (2411.06559#s2)
- Accessibility tree available for element-level walks; structural (MinHash) plus visual (pHash) deduplication (2604.02345#s1)
- Atomic transition space is relatively finite so goal-free exploration covers much of it (author premise) (2604.02345#s1)

## When it fails
- Dense text-heavy viewports (Reddit) do not benefit; gains taper with data scale (2411.06559#s2)
- No ablation of filtering stages or caption quality; scaling not monotone for every backbone (2604.02345#s1)

## Counterexamples
- none found in the readings
