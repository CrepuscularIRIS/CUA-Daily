# K05 — Train or fine-tune the policy on world-model rollouts, anchored with real data
_parent: **Learn GUI Dynamics and Train in Them** ([`learn_and_train_in_gui_world_model`](../main/learn_and_train_in_gui_world_model.md)) · status: candidate_pattern · source card: `K05-train-in-imagination-with-real-anchor.md` (frozen domain `cua-agents/v1`)_

## Operation
roll out the policy inside a learned world model (short horizons), score/filter the imagined trajectories (self-assessed reward, rule filters, rejection sampling), and train on them mixed with real expert or public trajectories

- Transfer: Dyna/Dreamer analogue. Transfer requires that the GUI world model's error on the states the policy visits stays small; real-data mixing acts as the regulariser that pure-imagination robotics methods get from model accuracy.

- Status: candidate_pattern

## When it works
- Short dream horizon (best 4-5 steps) (2601.22149#s2)
- A substantial fraction of real expert trajectories mixed in (~40% gives most of the gain) (2601.22149#s2, 2608.05891#s3)
- World model fine-tuned on the target environment family (2601.22149#s2, 2602.14721#s2)
- Return gap bounded by eps_rew/(1-gamma) plus a Lipschitz-weighted eps_dyn term (deterministic, contraction regime) (2605.06732#s1)
- Structured (object-centric) latent state supports imagination training in relational tasks (2410.08822#s2)

## When it fails
- Purely imagined training underperforms SFT / lowers ATP below the base model (2601.22149#s2, 2608.05891#s3)
- Long dreams degrade through compounding hallucination; only ~1/10 world-model rollouts pass quality checks (2601.22149#s2, 2608.05891#s3)
- Gains saturate (~20K rollouts); diversity bounded by world-model experience; consensus reward is weak (2608.05891#s3)
- No comparison against equal-size synthesis in the real environment (2602.14721#s2)
- The theoretical bound is loose by 1-2 orders of magnitude at the median (2605.06732#s1)

- none found in the readings
