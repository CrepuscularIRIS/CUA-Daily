# C08 — Supplement a low-resolution global view with a cheap high-resolution detail channel
_parent: **Localise Small Targets with Resolution, Zoom, Verification and Pointer Heads** ([`high_resolution_target_localisation`](../main/high_resolution_target_localisation.md)) · status: candidate_pattern · source card: `K08-highres-detail-channel.md` (frozen domain `cua-agents-core/v1`, where it is K08) · domain: cua-agents-core_

## Operation

- keep the backbone's low-resolution global image and add high-resolution detail through a separate path: a small high-res encoder read by cross-attention (CogAgent) or aspect-ratio grid sub-images encoded separately (Ferret-UI anyres).
- Status: candidate_pattern (2 full-text paper(s)).
- Transfer notes: n/a

## When it works

- Cross-attention to a small high-res encoder cuts attention cost (>10x FLOPs reduction at 1120) while matching higher-resolution baselines on text-heavy VQA. [2312.08914#s1]
- Sub-image splitting helps small targets on elongated mobile screens (iPhone advanced tasks 73.4 -> 93.9; fewer small icons misread as text). [2404.05719#s1]

## When it fails

- Architecture gain alone on Mind2Web is small (40.7 -> 41.4) compared with data gains; coordinates stay imprecise. [2312.08914#s1]
- Anyres reduced Android advanced-task (80.5 -> 70.1) and taperception scores; token cost not reported; not tested on desktop/web. [2404.05719#s1]
