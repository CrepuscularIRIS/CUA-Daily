# C35 — Pause sensitive actions for user confirmation, judged by the policy itself
_parent: **Defend Agents Against Untrusted Observations: Prompting, Gating and Isolation** ([`injection_defense_isolation`](../main/injection_defense_isolation.md)) · status: case · source card: `K35-human-confirmation-gating.md` (frozen domain `cua-agents-core/v1`, where it is K35) · domain: cua-agents-core_

## Operation

- have the policy label actions it deems sensitive or irreversible as PENDING and wait for user confirmation.
- Status: case (1 full-text paper(s)).
- Transfer notes: n/a

## When it works

- UFO requests confirmation on 85.7% of benign sensitive requests vs 50-57% for GPT baselines. [2402.07939#s4]

## When it fails

- Per-app rates as low as 50%; same policy decides; no adversarial content tested. [2402.07939#s4]

## Counterexamples

- Operator's built-in confirmation/safety checks cut ASR 30.89% -> 7.57% under injection, conditional on an attentive human. [2505.21936#s3]
