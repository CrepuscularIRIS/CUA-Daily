# K26 — Build error-correction and post-error recovery training data at the first mistake
_parent: **Train the Policy on Own, Replayed and Corrected Trajectories** ([`trajectory_level_policy_training`](../main/trajectory_level_policy_training.md)) · status: candidate_pattern · source card: `K26-first-error-correction-recovery-data.md` (frozen domain `cua-agents/v1`)_

## Operation
locate the first erroneous step of an agent trajectory (human or MLLM), create a corrected action for that step and a reflection/recovery action for the step after the error (or synthesise errors from successful trajectories), and train with SFT and/or DPO

- Status: candidate_pattern

## When it works
- Online rollouts in resettable environments with verifiers; first-error localisation available (2506.08012#s2, 2501.12326#s1)
- Adding reflection data raises level-2 success 14.58% -> 23.61%; online reflection tuning 23.61% -> 34.72% (2506.08012#s1, 2506.08012#s2)
- DPO on corrected vs erroneous actions improves OSWorld (72B 18.8 -> 22.7) (2501.12326#s1)

## When it fails
- Covers visual/grounding errors, not high-level planning errors; mobile-only (2506.08012#s1)
- Assumes Back restores the previous screen; MLLM verifier/first-error labels not validated against humans (2506.08012#s1, 2506.08012#s2)
- No ablation isolating reflection tuning in UI-TARS (2501.12326#s1)

## Counterexamples
- none found in the readings
