# K02 — Rerank sampled actions or trajectories with a learned/agentic verifier (no simulation)
_parent: **Select Actions by Simulated or Verified Lookahead** ([`simulated_lookahead_selection`](../main/simulated_lookahead_selection.md)) · status: candidate_pattern · source card: `K02-verifier-rerank-candidates.md` (frozen domain `cua-agents/v1`)_

## Operation
sample N candidate actions (per step) or N whole trajectories, score each with a separately trained or agentic verifier, and execute/submit the top-scored one

- Transfer: Distinct from K01: no next-state prediction, the verifier judges the action or trajectory directly; step-level variant needs no lookahead, trajectory-level variant needs N full rollouts and resettable state.

- Status: candidate_pattern

## When it works
- Verifier trained on high-quality (human) labels; GPT-4o-labelled PRMs give smaller gains (2509.23738#s2)
- Verifier accuracy well above 50%; App D derives Best-of-N gain as a function of actor SR and verifier accuracy (2602.00575#s2)
- Terminal environment state preserved per rollout for trajectory-level selection (2602.00575#s2)

## When it fails
- Gains plateau as N grows while compute grows linearly (2509.23738#s2)
- Near-chance verifier accuracy yields negligible or negative Best-of-N gain (theorem assumes symmetric accuracy) (2602.00575#s2)

## Counterexamples
- none found in the readings
