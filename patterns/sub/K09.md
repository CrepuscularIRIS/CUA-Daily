# K09 — Calibrate a digital twin with sparse real measurements and debias its synthetic data
_parent: **Calibrate a Simulator or Digital Twin to the Real Target** ([`calibrate_simulator_to_real`](../main/calibrate_simulator_to_real.md)) · status: case · source card: `K09-twin-calibration-and-synthetic-debiasing.md` (frozen domain `cua-agents/v1`)_

## Operation
calibrate simulator parameters by gradient through a differentiable twin (EM over latent errors), train on posterior-sampled twins, and debias synthetic training losses with a small real set (prediction-powered inference, context-aware bias terms)

- Transfer: Single review paper (telecom). Transfer to GUI twins: estimate per-context (per-page/per-app-state) bias of twin-derived rewards/data with a small set of real rollouts.

- Status: case

## When it works
- Small real validation set from the specific deployment; differentiable simulator (2507.07067#s1, 2507.07067#s3)
- Context features that separate regions where the twin is wrong (2507.07067#s3)

## When it fails
- Calibration cannot fully remove the gap of a limited-complexity simulator; twins are site-specific (2507.07067#s1)
- Plain PPI with a single global bias underperforms real-only training where the twin is wrong (NLoS) (2507.07067#s3)
- Bayesian twin training hinges on prior/model choice and costs more compute (2507.07067#s2)
