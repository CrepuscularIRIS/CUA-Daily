# C25 — Score agent trajectories with an LLM judge
_parent: **Reward with Learned Outcome and Process Judges** ([`learned_outcome_and_process_judges`](../main/learned_outcome_and_process_judges.md)) · status: candidate_pattern · source card: `K25-llm-trajectory-judge.md` (frozen domain `cua-agents-core/v1`, where it is K25) · domain: cua-agents-core_

## Operation

- prompt an (M)LLM with the task, the agent's answer and trajectory screenshots or an action/reasoning trace to emit success and safety labels.
- Status: candidate_pattern (2 full-text paper(s)).
- Transfer notes: n/a

## When it works

- Full-trajectory screenshots raise GPT-4V agreement with humans to kappa 0.70, matching inter-annotator agreement. [2401.13919#s3]
- Asymmetric safety prompts on a compact trace format reach safety precision 95% / recall 64%. [2506.14866#s2]

## When it fails

- Self-preference bias across judge models; live drift. [2401.13919#s3]
- Judge counts partial progress as success and is fooled by injection phrasing. [2506.14866#s2]

## Counterexamples

- None recorded.
