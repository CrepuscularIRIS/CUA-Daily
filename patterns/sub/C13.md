# C13 — Prune and restructure the text observation (a11y tree / control list) before the policy sees it
_parent: **Expose the Screen as Indexed Elements and Pruned Structure** ([`structured_screen_observation`](../main/structured_screen_observation.md)) · status: candidate_pattern · source card: `K13-text-observation-pruning.md` (frozen domain `cua-agents-core/v1`, where it is K13) · domain: cua-agents-core_

## Operation

- drop low-relevance nodes or control types and restructure repetitive structure (merge StaticText into elements, tables/lists to Markdown; type-filtered controls and agent-requested re-annotation of a shortlist).
- Status: candidate_pattern (2 full-text paper(s)).
- Transfer notes: n/a

## When it works

- Rule-based a11y-tree refinement raised WebArena SR 31.7% -> 37.1% while cutting tokens per step. [2410.13825#s3]
- Type filtering and shortlist re-annotation reduce clutter when UIA reports hundreds of controls (author claim). [2402.07939#s3]

## When it fails

- GitLab SR dropped 35.0 -> 26.7 after refinement; visual-only content is out of scope for text-only agents. [2410.13825#s3]
- No metric attributed to UFO's filtering. [2402.07939#s3]

## Counterexamples

- None recorded.
