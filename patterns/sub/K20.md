# K20 — Online RL on sparse terminal reward with learned value baselines and filtered/replayed data
_parent: **Train the Policy on Own, Replayed and Corrected Trajectories** ([`trajectory_level_policy_training`](../main/trajectory_level_policy_training.md)) · status: candidate_pattern · source card: `K20-advantage-filtered-online-rl.md` (frozen domain `cua-agents/v1`)_

## Operation
train step- and instruction-level critics with cross-entropy on the terminal (judge) reward, compute mixed TD/MC advantages, and update the actor only on advantageous or perplexity-filtered data (hard-filtered AWR, or KL-regularised off-policy updates with replay of successes)

- Status: candidate_pattern

## When it works
- Initialisation from SFT/offline data; ~1k online trajectories suffice on short AitW tasks (2406.11896#s1, 2411.02337#s3)
- KL constraint to the previous-phase policy and replay of successful trajectories in a perplexity band (2411.02337#s3)
- Curriculum/filtering of informative tasks (2406.11896#s1)

## When it fails
- Without KL or replay, performance degrades over phases; replaying failures destabilises (2411.02337#s3)
- Short tasks only; few repeats; evaluated on subsets (WebArena-Lite) (2406.11896#s1, 2411.02337#s3)

## Counterexamples
- none found in the readings
