# C31 — Perturb a trigger image to steer captions or the VLM's reading of it
_parent: **Hijack Agents Through the Environment and Measure Attempt vs Completion** ([`environmental_injection_attacks`](../main/environmental_injection_attacks.md)) · status: case · source card: `K31-adversarial-image-perturbation.md` (frozen domain `cua-agents-core/v1`, where it is K31) · domain: cua-agents-core_

## Operation

- optimise an L-inf-bounded perturbation on an attacker-owned image, white-box against an open captioner or transfer via a CLIP ensemble against a black-box VLM.
- Status: case (1 full-text paper(s)).
- Transfer notes: n/a

## When it works

- A white-box captioner raises image access to text-level threat (92% adversarial captions, 31-67% ASR). [2406.12814#s3]

## When it fails

- Black-box transfer drops sharply when the image is small in the screenshot and text describes the item (10% ASR). [2406.12814#s4]
- Needs a white-box captioner in the pipeline for the strong attack. [2406.12814#s3]

## Counterexamples

- None recorded.
