# C33 — Separate hijack attempts from attack completion in security evaluation
_parent: **Hijack Agents Through the Environment and Measure Attempt vs Completion** ([`environmental_injection_attacks`](../main/environmental_injection_attacks.md)) · status: candidate_pattern · source card: `K33-hijack-vs-completion-eval.md` (frozen domain `cua-agents-core/v1`, where it is K33) · domain: cua-agents-core_

## Operation

- score attacks both by executable final-state checks of the adversarial goal and by an intermediate measure (LLM-judged attempt / compromise, or task-level any-click rate), optionally starting episodes at the injection point.
- Status: candidate_pattern (3 full-text paper(s)).
- Transfer notes: n/a

## When it works

- Reveals that hijacking is frequent while completion is limited by capability (ASR-intermediate up to 0.857 vs end-to-end <= 0.167; AR up to 92.45%). [2504.18575#s3, 2505.21936#s2]
- Task-level ASR shows weak per-step attacks compound over multi-step tasks. [2411.02391#s4]

## When it fails

- LLM judge reliability not quantified and judges may themselves be susceptible. [2504.18575#s3, 2505.21936#s2]
- Decoupled starts overstate real-world ASR relative to end-to-end runs. [2505.21936#s2]

## Counterexamples

- None recorded.
