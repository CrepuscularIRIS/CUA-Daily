# R01 — Offline single-step GRPO with rule-checked action and grounding rewards
_parent: **Reinforce Single-Step Actions with Rule-Checked Rewards** ([`rule_reward_single_step_rl`](../main/rule_reward_single_step_rl.md)) · status: candidate_pattern · domain: board-induced (2026-10-19), from wave-1 weak fits_

## Operation
take static (screenshot or HTML, instruction, history) states with one labelled action from existing grounding/trajectory datasets, filter them by sampling the base model several times (drop all-correct / all-wrong or keep only failed "hard" samples), sample a group of responses per state, score each with hand-written rules against the label (format tag; action-type exact match; click point inside the box, distance-to-centre, box-size or IoU term; exact or semantic match of text/element arguments), and update with GRPO (often no or small KL), optionally after an SFT warm-up

- Transfer: R1-Zero-style rule-reward RL moved from math to GUI step prediction; no environment, rollout or terminal reward is needed, so it transfers to any dataset with a per-step label, but the reward is only as good as the single reference action.

- Status: candidate_pattern (7 member papers, 6 with full text)

## When it works
- A few hundred to a few thousand filtered samples suffice for large single-step gains over the zero-shot base: 136 samples lift ScreenSpot 65.0 -> 83.3 and AndroidControl type/grounding 79.3/72.3 -> 94.3/82.6 at 3B; 3K samples give 3B ScreenSpot 80.08 vs 63.55 and ScreenSpot-Pro 25.23 vs 13.80 for SFT on the same 3K; 17K samples reach ScreenSpot 90.3 / ScreenSpot-Pro 37.1; 3,018 samples reach ScreenSpot-Pro 47.3 at 7B (2503.21620#board, 2504.10458#board, 2505.15810#board, 2505.12370#board)
- Training states are filtered by base-model sampling and the filter is ablated: difficulty-selected data beats random selection and the full set; filtered GUI-R1-3K converges faster to a higher ceiling than unfiltered data; filtering plus dense reward gives ScreenSpot-Pro 40.0 vs 27.1 unfiltered with sparse reward (2503.21620#board, 2504.10458#board, 2505.12370#board)
- The location reward is shaped to the evaluation criterion and regularised: a point-in-box coordinate reward beats an IoU box reward; a dense distance reward adds 4.21 points over sparse 0/1; a box-size term counteracts the opposite size hacking of hit and IoU rewards (2503.21620#board, 2505.12370#board, 2505.15810#board, 2506.09373#board)
- Grounding is trained without long reasoning: longer chains lower grounding accuracy, and DAST then NOTHINK training gives ScreenSpot-Pro 33.5 vs 17.8 for the thinking variant (2505.15810#board, 2503.21620#board)
- For multi-step web tasks, an SFT warm-up precedes GRPO and RL is compared with SFT on the same backbone: WorkArena 14B 46.79 vs 30.20 SFT (GPT-4o 42.65), 3B 36.85 vs 26.59, and the gain holds on a second backbone (Llama-3.1-8B 32.41 vs 20.48 SFT) (2505.22942#board)

## When it fails
- Training and evidence are single-step and offline: states are scored by exact match to one reference action (used "as an effective surrogate for real-world verification"), multi-step tasks are cut into independent single-step problems, and single-image non-interactive training shows no "aha moment" (2505.22942#board, 2504.10458#board, 2503.21620#board)
- Shaped rewards are hacked: hit rewards shrink boxes and IoU rewards inflate them; text-similarity and piecewise-dense rewards drive the policy to repeat the most common action or emit tokens after the action tag; GRPO's length normalisation favours long incorrect answers; the action-type reward does not always help grounding (2505.15810#board, 2505.22942#board, 2503.21620#board)
- The RL contribution is confounded with data and backbone: R1-style agents differ in backbone, data and protocol, gains are compared with SFT models trained on far larger different data (3K vs 13M-14M), and an OS-subset result (16.1 vs 16.8 for the same data) suggests post-training activates pretrained knowledge (2505.15810#board, 2504.10458#board, 2505.12370#board)
- Icons stay weak on professional screens (ScreenSpot-Pro icon 16.8 vs text 49.5; 21.0 vs 63.5) (2505.15810#board, 2505.12370#board)
- On high-level tasks the gain over the zero-shot base is small (GUI-R1-7B AndroidControl-High/GUI-Odyssey overall 56.13 vs 50.53); only 3B/7B are tested; the method depends on large, precisely annotated grounding data and costs ~300 H100 GPU hours (2504.10458#board, 2505.12370#board, 2506.09373#board)

## Counterexamples
- Reward density cuts both ways: a dense distance reward helps grounding (+4.21) while dense text-similarity rewards cause reward hacking on WorkArena, where sparse exact-match reward is the most stable (2505.12370#board, 2505.22942#board)
- Reasoning helps on web navigation but hurts grounding: long-reasoning SFT and growing response length go with better WorkArena success, whereas grounding papers remove thinking (2505.22942#board, 2505.15810#board, 2503.21620#board)
