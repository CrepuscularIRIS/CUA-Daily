# C21 — Synthesize reasoning annotations for demonstrations with a strong MLLM, then SFT a screenshot policy
_parent: **Train the Policy on Own, Replayed and Corrected Trajectories** ([`trajectory_level_policy_training`](../main/trajectory_level_policy_training.md)) · status: candidate_pattern · source card: `K21-synthesized-reasoning-sft.md` (frozen domain `cua-agents-core/v1`, where it is K21) · domain: cua-agents-core_

## Operation

- have a proprietary model rewrite each demonstration step into plan/thought/reflection + grounded action (with hindsight before/after screenshots), and train on mixtures of reasoning levels.
- Status: candidate_pattern (2 full-text paper(s)).
- Transfer notes: n/a

## When it works

- Reflective long CoT raises OSWorld 11.5% -> 15.3% over short CoT; mixing L1/L2/L3 targets and decoding L2 reaches 18.5% vs 13.1% L2-only. [2508.09123#s2, 2508.09123#s3]
- GPT-4-rewritten plan+action targets with grounding data give CogAgent Mind2Web 58.2 step SR from screenshots. [2312.08914#s3]

## When it fails

- Self-correction is conjectured, not isolated; loops, termination misjudgement and one-character errors remain. [2508.09123#s2]
- L3 descriptions mislead; single runs with high online variance. [2508.09123#s3]
- Plan-rationale contribution not ablated; offline metric under-counts alternative correct paths (42% of sampled mismatches). [2312.08914#s3]

## Counterexamples

- None recorded.
