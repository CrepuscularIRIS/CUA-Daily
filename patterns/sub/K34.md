# K34 — Validate checkers two-sidedly: reject faulty, accept legal alternatives
_parent: **Ground Reward in Executable Checkers** ([`verifiable_execution_reward`](../main/verifiable_execution_reward.md)) · status: case · source card: `K34-two-sided-oracle-validation.md` (frozen domain `cua-agents/v1`)_

## Operation
assess a generated test/oracle on independent pools of faulty and legal implementations, and separate setup repair from behavioural-judgment revision in execution-feedback loops

- Transfer: Maps to GUI task checkers/judges (K12, K16): measure false-reject rate on alternative valid trajectories, not just detection of failures.

- Status: case

## When it works
- Trustworthy pools of legal and faulty implementations exist (2610.05001#s2)

## When it fails
- A recommendation from a survey, not measured (2610.05001#s2, 2610.05001#s3)

## Counterexamples
- none found in the readings
