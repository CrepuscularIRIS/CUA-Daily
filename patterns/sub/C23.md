# C23 — Offline step-level evaluation on recorded trajectories
_parent: **Make Online Evaluation Discriminating** ([`online_evaluation_methodology`](../main/online_evaluation_methodology.md)) · status: candidate_pattern · source card: `K23-offline-step-evaluation.md` (frozen domain `cua-agents-core/v1`, where it is K23) · domain: cua-agents-core_

## Operation

- replay cached states of human demonstrations and score each predicted step against annotated (possibly multiple) valid actions, aggregating to step SR.
- Status: candidate_pattern (2 full-text paper(s)).
- Transfer notes: n/a

## When it works

- Enables cheap evaluation of generalisation across task/site/domain splits. [2306.06070#s3]
- With multiple valid options per step, offline step SR tracks online OSWorld 15-step SR (power-law fit) and preserves rankings. [2508.09123#s5]

## When it fails

- An uncached action fails the task, giving false negatives when several paths exist; no recovery measured. [2306.06070#s3, 2508.09123#s5]
- Fine-tuned models score higher from action-space alignment; correlation on few points. [2508.09123#s5]

## Counterexamples

- Online success far exceeds strict offline success and reorders models (SeeAct 37.8 online vs 3.3 offline). [2401.01614#s4]
- 42% of sampled AITW mismatches were alternative correct paths. [2312.08914#s3]
