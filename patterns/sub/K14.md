# K14 — Relabel exploration trajectories with retroactively inferred instructions
_parent: **Synthesise Tasks from Exploration and History** ([`exploration_driven_task_synthesis`](../main/exploration_driven_task_synthesis.md)) · status: candidate_pattern · source card: `K14-hindsight-relabel-exploration.md` (frozen domain `cua-agents/v1`)_

## Operation
explore the environment without a fixed task, then infer an instruction that the observed transitions/trajectory accomplish (hindsight/reverse task synthesis), filter with an LLM ORM, optionally re-execute the instruction, and SFT on the resulting pairs

- Transfer: Hindsight experience relabelling (HER) analogue.

- Status: candidate_pattern

## When it works
- Interaction-first beats instruction-first at equal budget (MiniWoB 0.48 vs 0.28; WebArena 7.2 vs 4.2) (2410.02907#s3)
- Instructions reference deep, site-specific entities discovered by exploration (2410.02907#s3, 2412.19723#s1)
- Strong LLM/VLM for relabelling and execution (2410.02907#s1, 2412.19723#s1)

## When it fails
- Saturation with scale, bounded by executor capability (2412.19723#s1)
- Poor cross-site transfer; mixing live and WebArena data hurts 7 sites (2410.02907#s2)
- Synthesis environments overlap the evaluation environments (2412.19723#s1)
- Live-site exploration cannot log in or submit content (2410.02907#s1)

## Counterexamples
- none found in the readings
