# K11 — Use the twin to harvest or target demonstrations, retrain with real data as anchor
_parent: **Calibrate a Simulator or Digital Twin to the Real Target** ([`calibrate_simulator_to_real`](../main/calibrate_simulator_to_real.md)) · status: candidate_pattern · source card: `K11-twin-targeted-demos-with-real-cotraining.md` (frozen domain `cua-agents/v1`)_

## Operation
run the current policy in the twin to collect successful rollouts or find failure states, add targeted (sim) demonstrations there, and retrain/distil while co-training on the original real demonstrations

- Transfer: GUI analogue: replay the agent in an app twin, find failure states, collect corrective demonstrations there, keep real trajectories in the mixture (cf. K05 real anchoring).

- Status: candidate_pattern

## When it works
- Real policy transfers to the twin well enough to produce some successes (2403.03949#s2)
- Real-demo co-training during distillation (3.5x/2x over sim co-training) (2403.03949#s3)
- Kinematic tasks where the twin corrects poses; 30 targeted sim demos ~= 30 extra real demos (2504.03597#s2)

## When it fails
- Step-function dependence on demo count: 0% when real policy fails in sim (2403.03949#s2)
- Sim-only demos can include behaviour that breaks synchronisation at deployment (2504.03597#s2)
- ~2 days wall-clock per task (2403.03949#s3)

## Counterexamples
- none found in the readings
