# K08 — Learn a residual correction that makes a simulator reproduce logged real trajectories
_parent: **Calibrate a Simulator or Digital Twin to the Real Target** ([`calibrate_simulator_to_real`](../main/calibrate_simulator_to_real.md)) · status: candidate_pattern · source card: `K08-residual-sim-correction-from-real-logs.md` (frozen domain `cua-agents/v1`)_

## Operation
replay logged real state-action segments in the simulator from recorded start states and learn a residual (force or delta-action) model that makes the simulated next states match the real ones, then train/fine-tune the policy in the corrected simulator

- Transfer: Maps to GUI digital twins calibrated from real interaction: logged real GUI trajectories would play the role of MoCap clips, and a residual would correct a hand-built or generated app simulator where its transitions disagree with real logs. Requires the GUI twin to be resettable to logged real states and the mismatch to be expressible as a local correction.

- Status: candidate_pattern

## When it works
- Simulator resettable to recorded real states and real states measurable (MoCap/markers) (2502.01143#s1, 2402.01086#s1)
- Gap not explained by a few physical parameters: residual beats parameter SysID, which yields implausible parameters and little gain (2402.01086#s2, 2502.01143#s1)
- RL fine-tuning in the corrected simulator beats training-free uses of the correction model (2502.01143#s2)

## When it fails
- Out-of-distribution regimes (1.75x pressure) break the residual; autoregressive error accumulates (2402.01086#s1)
- Data-hungry: full 23-DoF delta model impractical, reduced to 4-DoF; MoCap dependence (2502.01143#s1)
- At short horizons not better than open-loop/SysID (2502.01143#s1)

## Counterexamples
- none found in the readings
