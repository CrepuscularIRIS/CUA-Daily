# C07 — Zoom-and-reground search for targets on high-resolution professional screens
_parent: **Localise Small Targets with Resolution, Zoom, Verification and Pointer Heads** ([`high_resolution_target_localisation`](../main/high_resolution_target_localisation.md)) · status: case · source card: `K07-zoom-reground-highres.md` (frozen domain `cua-agents-core/v1`, where it is K07) · domain: cua-agents-core_

## Operation

- ground on the full screenshot, then crop around the prediction (or around planner-proposed areas scored by grounded neighbour votes) and ground again recursively.
- Status: case (1 full-text paper(s)).
- Transfer notes: n/a

## When it works

- Cropping around the first prediction more than doubles accuracy for off-the-shelf grounders (OS-Atlas-7B 18.9 -> 40.2) with crop size tuned to the grounder's input resolution. [2504.07981#s2]
- A planner with GUI layout priors naming areas, target and neighbours raises accuracy to 48.1% without training. [2504.07981#s3]

## When it fails

- Fails when the first prediction is far off (locked onto a background-window tab); crops too small lose context, too large exceed capacity. [2504.07981#s2]
- Icon accuracy stays low (22.4%); cost/latency of multiple planner and grounder calls not reported; relies on planner layout priors. [2504.07981#s3]
