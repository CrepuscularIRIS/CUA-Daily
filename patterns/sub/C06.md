# C06 — Crop the proposed target and verify it against the goal before acting
_parent: **Localise Small Targets with Resolution, Zoom, Verification and Pointer Heads** ([`high_resolution_target_localisation`](../main/high_resolution_target_localisation.md)) · status: candidate_pattern · source card: `K06-crop-verify-target.md` (frozen domain `cua-agents-core/v1`, where it is K06) · domain: cua-agents-core_

## Operation

- crop the screenshot around a candidate click target (optionally marking the point), ask a verifier model whether the crop shows the goal, and act on the first verified candidate or re-ground by scanning other element crops.
- Status: candidate_pattern (2 full-text paper(s)).
- Transfer notes: Requires that the goal is visually verifiable on a local crop and that element geometry is trustworthy.

## When it works

- Raises high-resolution grounding when the grounder emits diverse candidates in one pass (ScreenSpot-Pro +3.5 to +12.9). [2506.03143#s3]
- As a security gate trusting geometry but not structural text, it cuts text-swap hijack from 0.93-0.98 to 0.28-0.43 on MiniWoB++ with aligned error within 0.04 of baseline. [2607.04334#s4]

## When it fails

- Small or negative effect on easier benchmarks (ScreenSpot Web-Icon 85.4 -> 80.6 for 2B) and adds sequential verifier calls per candidate. [2506.03143#s3]
- On dense AndroidWorld screens repair is partial (hijack 0.89 -> 0.77) at 3.3-5.5 queries per action; coordinate-swap attacks out of scope. [2607.04334#s4]

## Counterexamples

- None recorded.
